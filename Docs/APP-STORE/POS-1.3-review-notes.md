Rabbitty POS is a companion business tool for merchants and affiliated venue managers (restaurants, coffee shops) to process orders, manage tables, and grant loyalty points (bunz) at checkout.

Demo tenant: "Demo Lead Café" (Sandbox Demo). It contains only fictional demo data — no real business data is included.

Review Instructions:
1. Open Rabbitty POS on iPad or Mac.
2. Sign in with the demo account:
   - Email: pos-reviewer@rabbitty.me
   - Code: 123456 (fixed for review)
3. If the POS asks to link the terminal, tap "Vincular esta terminal" and name it "Caja Demo".
4. Open the register shift with a demo PIN:
   - Manager: 1234
   - Cashier: 5678
5. Add menu items to the ticket and charge with "Efectivo" (Cash). The receipt preview opens and the reward QR is displayed for customers who are not linked to a Rabbitty account.
6. The "Mesas" (Tables) view shows the floor plan statuses. "Corte de Caja" shows the cash drawer, shift totals and the Z closing.
7. The "Modo Entrenamiento (Sandbox)" toggle lets you test orders without affecting live accounting.

Permissions Used:
- Bluetooth (LE): optional, used to connect a thermal receipt/kitchen printer.
- Camera: used exclusively to scan customer loyalty QR codes (Bunz rewards).
